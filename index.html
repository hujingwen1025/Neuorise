Adaptive therapeutic sound

Healing music shaped around your current state.

Neuorise turns emotional check-ins, heart rate, breathing rate, and listening feedback into saved music-generation sessions with Gemini and Suno integration points ready for production APIs.

Start a new session

Product flow

A private loop for better listening

Users must sign up before creating tracks, so every questionnaire, generated prompt, track version, and feedback note is saved to their own account.

1. Survey

Collect mood, needs, preferences, heart rate, and respiratory rate.

2. Gemini prompt

Server-side Gemini prepares a therapeutic Suno prompt.

3. Suno generation

Suno creates track metadata, task status, and final audio.

4. Feedback

Thumbs, skips, and notes create new saved track versions.
